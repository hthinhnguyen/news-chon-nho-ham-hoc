chon-nho-ham-hoc

A private, single-user tool that publishes short news videos to the YouTube channel Chồn nhỏ ham học, owned and operated by Nguyễn Hữu Thịnh.

What it does

  1. Reads public RSS feeds of Vietnamese news sites and writes an original short script summarising one article, with a link to the source.
  2. Renders a vertical video (voice-over, subtitles, the article's images) on the owner's own computer.
  3. Uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3 (videos.insert) as private, then checks its status (videos.list).
  4. The owner reviews every video in YouTube Studio and makes it public manually.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any other user's YouTube account. It runs on the owner's personal computer and signs in with Google OAuth 2.0.
